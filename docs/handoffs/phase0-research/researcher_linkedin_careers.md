Worked well: direct fetch of Greenhouse/SmartRecruiters/Personio/company career pages, and LinkedIn public /jobs/view/ pages (Doctolib job fetched clean). WebSearch was essential to find live URLs since search-cached job pages go stale fast.
Failed/blocked: LinkedIn job search results are heavily cached with stale IDs (most returned 404/expired on fetch) — direct LinkedIn fetch works only when the exact live job ID is known. Surprising observation 1: Job aggregator "hiring pipeline" postings rot fast — most 200-day-old search results for named companies (Zalando, N26, Contentful, GetYourGuide) point to already-closed reqs; had to re-search with "current"/2026 qualifiers repeatedly to land on live ones.
Surprising observation 2: Consultancy/enterprise "AI Engineer" titles (SAP, Siemens, Capgemini) list far longer, more concrete agent-framework stacks (LangChain, LangGraph, AutoGen, CrewAI, Semantic Kernel, multiple cloud LLM providers) than most Berlin startup postings, which lean on 1-2 frameworks plus "production LLM experience" language.
Surprising observation 3: Several Capgemini Invent "(Gen)AI & Data Strategy" consultant postings named zero concrete tools beyond "Technologien" and MS-Office despite AI in the title — excluded them as too vague to yield real requirement tags.
